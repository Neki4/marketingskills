Who We Are

Dublin's Most Trusted Attic Conversion Specialists

We're a Dublin-based team specialising exclusively in attic conversions across Dublin, Wicklow and Kildare. Every project managed from first survey to final handover — no subcontracting, no surprises.

From insulation and drylining to electrics, plumbing, plastering and flooring — we handle every aspect under one roof. Single point of contact and a fixed price from day one.

What We Have

We have an engineer who will carry out the necessary inspections and provide a written report on the structural work. We also have references from previous clients, and we can arrange for you to visit completed projects, both recent and older ones, with the owners' agreement, so you can see the quality of our work yourself.
